## How to use this site

1. **Work through a unit from top to bottom.** Definitions and lists in the courseware's exact words appear in quote blocks; learn those word for word. Explanations around them are there to help you understand.
2. **Answer the "Check your understanding" questions** as you go. Click an option to see whether you were right and why.
3. **Finish with the unit's end matter:** key terms, summary, mixed review, practice exercises and sample exam questions. Try each question before you open the model answer.
4. **Before the exam,** use the [crash course](exam/crash-course.md), the [exam technique](exam/exam-technique.md) page and the two mock exams.
